05 · Rameshwaram Fishermen FPO

Closer to the market. Fairer to the catch.

Formed in 2021 — I serve as Managing Director of the Rameshwaram Fishermen Farmer Producer Organisation.

The FPO connects fishermen directly with the fish market, reducing the role of intermediaries so that fishing families retain a greater share of the value generated from their catch.

Its focus is on strengthening market access and improving the economic position of fishing communities.
